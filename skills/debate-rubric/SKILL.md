# Debate Rubric

## severity
Rate objections 1-5: 1 nitpick; 2 advisory; 3 material and answerable; 4 blocking; 5 fatal (unfalsifiable, unexecutable, or contradicted by cited evidence).

## hard-gates
FAIL regardless of score for no falsifiable prediction, an unmeasurable outcome, any severity 4+, or 3+ unresolved statistics objections.

## protocol
Carry every objection across rounds. A dropped prior returns unresolved (fail-closed). The proposer must preempt recurring objection tags or explicitly concede them.
