# Evidence-Grounded Review

## Procedure
1. Apply the checklist and schema; distinguish deterministic failures from qualitative judgment.
2. Never waive citation, statistics, provenance, or contract gates for a model score.
3. Tie each finding to a claim/section, failed rule or evidence, severity, and minimal correction.
4. Check falsifiability, source and method fit, negatives, controls, uncertainty, reproducibility, and limitations.
5. Mark missing evidence; never invent citations. Repairs preserve passing text and rerun failed checks.

## Output Check
Findings are actionable; hard gates and evidence lineage remain intact.
