# Statistical Interpretation

## Procedure
1. Use reports and raw artifacts as the numeric source; never invent or recompute prose values.
2. State n and distinguish supplied SD, SE, or CI. Match conclusions to the preregistered prediction, comparator, metric, test, and correction.
3. Separate p-value, effect size, and practical importance. Label supported, falsified, or inconclusive by the stated rule.
4. Missing/underpowered evidence is not positive. Preserve failures and limits; do not exceed causal scope.

## Output Check
Numbers and verdict match evidence; claim scope fits the design.
