# Falsifiable Research Design

## Procedure
1. Turn each claim into an observable prediction with outcome, comparator, population/data, and decision window.
2. Define support, falsification, and inconclusive outcomes before seeing results.
3. Name the strongest rival explanation and the baseline/control that tests it.
4. Match measures, sample/seed plan, uncertainty, test, and multiplicity correction to the prediction; never choose tests for significance.
5. For causal claims, state estimand, identification assumptions, confounding risks, and robustness checks. Otherwise use associational scope.
6. Check dataset, compute, sandbox, and contract feasibility; expose blockers.

## Output Check
Every contribution has a falsifiable prediction, failure condition, relevant controls, and feasible measurement plan.
