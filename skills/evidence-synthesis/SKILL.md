# Evidence Synthesis

## Procedure
1. Use retrieved records as the evidence boundary; separate reported findings, methods, population, and limitations.
2. Link claims to supplied DOI/arXiv IDs or evidence records. Preserve conflicting results and distinguish evidence from inference.
3. State corpus/search limits. Describe gaps as bounded and testable; never infer "no prior work" from a narrow or failed search.
4. For screening/extraction, apply supplied criteria and schema exactly. Mark uncertain or unavailable fields; do not guess.

## Output Check
Claims are traceable, counter-evidence and coverage limits are visible, and output matches the requested schema.
